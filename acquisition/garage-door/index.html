Common questions.

1. What do I actually get?

Booked estimate appointments. Homeowners in your area who own their home, have a failing or dated door, and have already agreed to a time for you to come out and quote the job. Real appointments on your calendar, not a list of phone numbers to chase or people who are only curious.

2. Is this just running ads?

No. The ads are only the first step. Around them I build the part that actually turns interest into a booked job: the message that gets homeowners to reach out, the page they land on, and the screening that puts only the serious buyers on your calendar. Running ads without all that is why most contractors waste money.

3. I've tried Facebook ads before and they didn't work.

That's the usual story, and it's almost never the ad itself. Hitting boost on a post sends people nowhere. No offer, no page to send them to, and nothing sorting the serious buyers from the merely curious. I build all of that, and that's what turns the money into real jobs.

4. What does it cost me? Do I pay for the ads too?

Two separate things, and I keep them clear. The ad money goes straight to Facebook to show your ads. It's yours, and it never comes to me. My fee is separate, and you only pay it when I actually deliver booked estimates. No estimates, no fee. Right now you also get my lowest rate while I build more case studies. We go through the exact numbers together on the call.

5. What if the leads are no good?

Then you don't pay for them. You only pay for estimates that meet the bar we set: a homeowner in your area, who owns the home, has a real need, and is booked on your calendar. Wrong-fit people and tire-kickers don't count toward your number, and they don't cost you.

6. How fast will I see estimates?

You'll see the first responses within days of going live. Booked estimates usually start landing in the first couple of weeks. The first month is where we find which message and which homeowners work best for you, so it starts steady and builds. Anyone promising a flood in week one is selling you a spike that won't last.

7. I'm not a computer person. Do I need a website?

No website needed, and you don't have to be techy at all. I handle every technical part: the ads, the pages, the setup, all of it. Your only job is to show up to the estimates I book and do what you already do best.

8. How much of my time does this take?

Very little. I run the whole thing. You do the installs I bring you, and we check in every couple of weeks so you can tell me what's closing and what isn't. The day-to-day is on me.

9. I already get enough work from referrals.

Good, and keep them. The trouble with referrals is they dry up in the slow months, right when you need the work. This gives you a steady flow you can turn up whenever you want more installs, with referrals still coming in on top.

10. If we stop working together, who keeps everything?

You do. I build everything on your own accounts, so the ads, the results, and all the information stay with you. You're never locked in, and you never lose what we built.
