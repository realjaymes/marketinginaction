Common questions.

1. Do I need to be running ads already?

No. If you're running ads, the audit will show you what's leaking. If you're not, the audit will show you what to set up first so you don't waste money the moment you start. Same $500 either way.

2. What if my budget for ads is small?

Small budgets work. You can run $5 to $20 a day to start testing creative, validating your offer with cold traffic, and gathering the data we need to make decisions. The retainer fee covers the system around the ads, the funnel, the creative, the follow-up, the tracking, not the ad spend itself. We scale spend deliberately once we see what's working. Even at $5 a day on Meta, you can ship a real campaign and learn fast.

3. Do you guarantee leads or sales?

No. Anyone who does is lying. Paid acquisition has too many variables outside any operator's control. What I guarantee: real work, real reporting, real execution to the standard described in the SOW. If I can't make progress in 90 days, you'll know early enough to make a decision.

4. Can I just buy the audit and skip the retainer?

Yes. The audit is a real deliverable on its own. About a third of audit clients take the document and execute themselves. About half move into the retainer. The rest hand the audit to someone else. All three are fine outcomes.

5. How much of my time will this take?

Strategy call: 30 minutes. Audit handoff: 30 minutes. Once on retainer: a 30-minute call every two to four weeks plus 15 minutes a week to approve creative and answer Slack messages. Less than an hour a week in total. You're not running this. I am.

6. How long until I see results?

Honest timeline: months 1 and 2 are learning. We're testing creative, building audience data, and finding what converts in your account. Performance is uneven by design. Month 3 is stabilization, CPL and ROAS start trending toward target. Months 4+ are optimization, steady improvement. If we're not making real progress by the end of the 3-month minimum, you'll know early enough to make a decision. Anyone telling you they'll get you results in week one is lying or selling you a one-week spike that won't repeat.
