Common questions.

1. Is this just running ads?

No. Ads are one part. What I install is the whole system around them: the offer, the funnel, the follow-up, the tracking, the creative, and the content that brings people in. Ads are just the traffic. Most acquisition fails because everything around the ads is missing, not because the ads themselves are bad. That is the part I build.

2. Do you handle the creative?

Yes, all of it, in your brand. I use AI to generate fresh branded carousels, static ads, and video every week, with the video fronted by an AI clone of you or a branded AI presenter, so you never wait on a shoot or run out of ads. One proven angle becomes dozens of ads, and the same creative powers your organic content and your paid ads. This is the part almost no one else does.

3. Do I need to be running ads already?

No. If you're running ads, the Diagnostic shows you what's leaking. If you're not, it shows you what to set up first so you don't waste money the moment you start. If you don't even have a website yet, that's fine too, we start by getting you online. Same Diagnostic either way.

4. Do I have to buy the whole system?

No. Some businesses need the whole engine. Some need one part: just the creative, just the content, or just getting online and set up to capture demand. The Diagnostic tells us which pieces you actually need and in what order, so you only pay to install what moves your business. Most clients start with one piece and grow into the system.

5. Do you guarantee leads or sales?

I won't promise you a magic number, because the last mile depends on you. What the system does is put your offer in front of the right people at a cost you can see, and bring in demand you can count on. For a service business or a coach, that shows up as leads and booked calls, and closing them is your part. For an ecommerce brand, the sale happens on the ad itself, so you see it as orders and return on ad spend. Either way I promise real work, real reporting, and real results. Run this properly and the customers come.

6. Can I just buy the Diagnostic and skip the rest?

Yes. The Diagnostic is a real deliverable on its own: a full document, a Loom walkthrough, and a build map. Take it and execute yourself, hand it to your team, or have me install it. If you do work with me, the fee comes off your install. All of those are fine outcomes.

7. Who owns the ad account, the data, and everything you build?

You do. Everything runs on your ad accounts, your pixel, your data. I build inside your accounts, not mine, so if we ever stop working together you keep all of it: the audiences, the creative, the tracking, the funnel. You are never locked in.

8. How long until I see results?

You can see the first signs early. Once the ads go live we start getting real responses, and the first customers and sales usually land within the first few weeks. The first month or two is where we test messages and audiences to find your winners, so it starts small and gets steadier as we go. From there it builds, more of what works and less of what doesn't. Anyone promising a flood of sales in week one is selling you a spike that won't last. What I focus on is real progress you can see early and grow every month.
