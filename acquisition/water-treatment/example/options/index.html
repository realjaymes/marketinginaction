Signs worth a test

Most water problems show up around the house first.

If any of these sound familiar, a test can show you the likely cause.

Spots on dishes and glasses

Cloudy film that comes back after every wash.

White crust on faucets

Scale on shower heads, faucets and the coffee maker.

Dry skin and dull hair

Soap that's hard to rinse off, and skin that feels dry after a shower.

A water heater working harder

Scale inside the tank makes it less efficient and wears it down over time.

Odd taste or smell

Chlorine from city water, or a rotten-egg smell from a well.

Orange or brown stains

Iron in well water stains sinks, tubs and laundry.

Questions

Before you book

Is the water test really free?

Yes. There's no charge for the test and no obligation to buy anything.

How long does it take?

About 30 minutes. Someone over 18 needs to be home.

Do you test well water?

Yes. We check the common well water problems too, like iron and sulfur.

What happens after the test?

You get your results there and then. If treatment would help, we explain the options and leave a written quote. If your water is fine, we'll tell you that.

Do I need to own the home?

Treatment equipment is installed in the home, so we test for homeowners. If you rent, your landlord can book the test.
