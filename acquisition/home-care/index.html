Common questions.

1. What do I actually get?

Booked consultation appointments. Families in your area who are the decision-maker, have a real and near-term need for care, can pay, and have already agreed to a time to talk with you. Real appointments on your calendar, not a list of phone numbers to chase or people who are only gathering information.

2. Is this just running ads?

No. The ads are only the first step. Around them I build the part that actually turns interest into a booked consultation: the message that gets families to reach out, the page they land on, and the screening that puts only the ready ones on your calendar. Running ads without all that is why most agencies waste money.

3. I've tried Facebook ads before and they didn't work.

That's the usual story, and it's almost never the ad itself. Boosting a post sends people nowhere. No offer, no page to send them to, and nothing sorting the ready families from the ones just looking. I build all of that, and that's what turns the money into booked consultations.

4. What does it cost me? Do I pay for the ads too?

Two separate things, and I keep them clear. The ad money goes straight to the platform to show your ads. It's yours, and it never comes to me. My fee is separate, and you only pay it for the qualified consultations I actually deliver. Right now you also get my lowest rate while I build more case studies. We go through the exact numbers together on the call.

5. What if the leads are no good?

Then you don't pay for them. You only pay for consultations that meet the bar we set: a family decision-maker in your area, with a real and near-term need, able to pay, booked on your calendar. The just-looking and the no-budget-yet don't count toward your number, and they don't cost you.

6. How fast will I see consultations?

You'll see the first responses within days of going live. Booked consultations usually start landing in the first couple of weeks. The first month is where we find which message and which families work best for you, so it starts steady and builds. Anyone promising a flood in week one is selling you a spike that won't last.

7. I already have a website. Do you replace it?

No. If you already have a good website and brand, we leave it alone. What most agencies are missing isn't a website, it's a paid engine that brings in families every month on purpose. That's the part I add and run for you, alongside everything you already have.

8. How much of my time does this take?

Very little. I run the whole thing. You do the consultations and the care I bring you, and we check in every couple of weeks so you can tell me what's converting and what isn't. The day-to-day is on me.

9. I already get enough clients from referrals.

Good, and keep them. The trouble with referrals is they dry up in the slow months, right when you have caregivers to keep busy. This gives you a steady flow you can turn up whenever you want more clients, with referrals still coming in on top.

10. If we stop working together, who keeps everything?

You keep your leads and your results, always. I run the ads on your account and the system on my side while we work together, and every family and every bit of data we gather is yours. You're never locked in, and you never lose what we built.
