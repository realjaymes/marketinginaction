Common questions.

1. Is this just running ads?

No. The ads are only the first step. Around them I build the part that actually turns interest into a booked job: the message that gets the right people to reach out, the page they land on, and the screening that puts only the serious buyers on your calendar. Running ads without all that is why most local businesses waste money.

2. What does it cost me? Do I pay for the ads too?

Two separate things, and I keep them clear. The ad money goes straight to Meta to show your ads. It's yours, and it never comes to me. My fee is separate, and you only pay it when I actually deliver qualified appointments. No appointments, no fee.

3. What if the leads are no good?

Then you don't pay for them. You only pay for appointments that meet the bar we set for your industry. Wrong-fit people and tire-kickers don't count toward your number, and they don't cost you.

4. I'm not a computer person. Do I need a website?

No website needed, and you don't have to be techy at all. I handle every technical part: the ads, the pages, the setup, all of it. Your only job is to show up to the appointments I book and do what you already do best.

5. I already get enough work from referrals.

Good, keep them. The trouble with referrals is they dry up in the slow months, right when you need the work. This gives you a steady flow you can turn up whenever you want more jobs, with referrals still coming in on top.

6. If we stop working together, who keeps everything?

You do. I build everything on your own accounts, so the ads, the results, and all the information stay with you. You're never locked in, and you never lose what we built.

Is your industry still open?

I work with a limited number of businesses per industry, one per service area, and every application is reviewed. Book a free strategy call and I'll tell you honestly whether yours is still open.
