Where these numbers come from
  • 3.4% reply rate. The 2026 business-to-business cold email benchmark average, down from 5% in 2025 and 8.5% in 2019. Top performers reach 8 to 12%.
  • 20% of replies are positive. An analysis of more than two million cold emails put the figure near 14%. Strong campaigns reach 50%. The page uses 20%.
  • 50% of positive replies become a held meeting. The rest cancel, no-show or go quiet.
  • 30 safe sends per mailbox per day. The conservative standard for cold sending from a warmed mailbox. Technical limits are far higher, deliverability limits are not.
  • Every tool price was read from the vendor's own pricing page rather than a comparison article, and each one is editable in the section below.
  • At these defaults, 2,100 contacts produce 7.1 held meetings. That is 0.34% of contacts, against a published contact-to-meeting range of 0.5% to 2.5%, so the funnel on this page sits below the benchmark rather than above it.

Verified 21 September 2026 Domain pricing, and why year two moves 10 extensions, first-year against renewal

Registrar pricing at Spaceship, checked live. The pattern is consistent across registrars even where the exact figures differ: the cheaper the first year, the steeper the renewal. This is the table behind the rotation toggle.

Extension First year Renewal Renewal premium Best for

The counterintuitive part. Cheap first-year extensions are only cheap if you rotate. Keep a .online for two years and it costs more than a .com. Flat renewers like .us, .net and .biz cost more on day one and less every year after, so the right extension is a function of the rotation toggle above, not a matter of taste.
