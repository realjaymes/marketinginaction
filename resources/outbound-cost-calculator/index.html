Step 6 — Automation

Smartlead sends. It does not tell you anything.

There is no "email me when a lead replies", and a campaign that auto-pauses on its own bounce protection does not notify you either. Two n8n workflows on Smartlead's webhooks fix both: one fires on a reply, one fires only when a campaign moves to paused. Registrar pricing at Spaceship, checked live. The pattern is consistent across registrars even where the exact figures differ: the cheaper the first year, the steeper the renewal. This is the table behind the rotation toggle.

Extension First year Renewal Renewal premium Best for

The counterintuitive part. Cheap first-year extensions are only cheap if you rotate. Keep a .online for two years and it costs more than a .com. Flat renewers like .us, .net and .biz cost more on day one and less every year after, so the right extension is a function of the rotation toggle above, not a matter of taste.
