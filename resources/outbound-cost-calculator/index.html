What a cold outbound system actually costs.

Most outbound budgets are built backwards, starting from a tool list. Send volume is what drives everything: it sets your inbox count, your inbox count sets your domain count, and your contact volume sets your verification and enrichment spend. Enter one number below and the whole stack prices itself. Every figure stays editable, because your rates are not the list rates.

This is the single most common reason an outbound engagement feels like it failed at week four. The client counts from the day they paid. The system counts from the first send, which is three weeks later. Both parties are looking at the same campaign and reading completely different results.

Verified 21 September 2026 Domain pricing, and why year two moves 10 extensions, first-year against renewal

Registrar pricing at Spaceship, checked live. The pattern is consistent across registrars even where the exact figures differ: the cheaper the first year, the steeper the renewal. This is the table behind the rotation toggle.

Extension First year Renewal Renewal premium Best for

The counterintuitive part. Cheap first-year extensions are only cheap if you rotate. Keep a .online for two years and it costs more than a .com. Flat renewers like .us, .net and .biz cost more on day one and less every year after, so the right extension is a function of the rotation toggle above, not a matter of taste.
