34% Trial Activation Lift From a Post-Acquisition SaaS Repositioning

Company Summary

Reform (SaaS)

Reform is a conversion-focused form builder SaaS for non-technical growth marketers in B2B and B2C lead-generation organizations to deploy multi-step, interactive forms that maximize lead quality and quantity.

Reform was acquired by FunnelEnvy in 2023, and was repositioned and remodeled from an easy-to-use, no-code general-purpose form builder to focus on lead conversion use cases, aiming to improve form conversion rates while minimizing low-quality responses and spam.

Engagement Summary

Challenge:

Following its acquisition by FunnelEnvy, Reform needed repositioning from a general-purpose form builder into a category-defining conversion-focused product, with a stronger PLG funnel, lifecycle automation, more disciplined GTM & feature launches, and expanded market presence through partnerships, SEO, and developer-focused positioning.

Solution:

I redefined its positioning, developed new landing pages, built a PLG funnel with improved free trials and lifecycle automation, implemented new GTM & feature launch processes, led email migration from Userlist to Brevo, executed the legacy freemium plan sunset, ran acquisition experiments across SEO, integrations and partnerships, and expanded Reform's case study presence and partner program foundations.

Results:

Delivered 34% uplift in trial activation, 21% improvement in trial-to-paid conversion, migrated 72% of legacy freemium users to paid plans, published content at 100x previous velocity, drove a 48% increase in non-branded organic traffic, positioned Reform competitively in CRO-focused SaaS, and laid the strategic foundation for builder-led distribution through GTM engineering positioning and a partner program framework.

"James played a critical role in Reform's repositioning and GTM execution, accelerating adoption while strengthening its role as a strategic growth driver for FunnelEnvy. His MIA newsletter was also the perfect platform to introduce Reform to non-technical growth marketers through founder interviews and in-depth MarTech product showcases that helped us articulate our value proposition and reach the right audience. His engaged community was also key to gathering valuable product feedback."

Summary Impact

Reform strengthened its PLG engine, accelerated organic acquisition, and improved product-led conversion through structured positioning, lifecycle automation, and AI-driven content systems. The engagement delivered:

  • 34% increase in trial activation and conversions
  • 21% improvement in trial-to-paid conversion through Pro Plan Free Trial campaigns
  • 72% of legacy freemium users migrated to paid plans through lifecycle automation
  • 100x increase in content velocity and 48% rise in non-branded organic traffic
  • 22% domain rating increase through AI-powered SEO and directory listings across 130+ platforms
  • 29% boost in referral traffic through marketplace listings
  • 19% more decision-stage leads through competitor comparison and integration SEO pages
  • Clear GTM processes increasing feature adoption by 26% and improving onboarding activation by 18%
  • A strategic foundation for builder-led distribution through GTM engineering positioning, a creator/agency partner incentive framework, and co-marketing playbooks with complementary GTM tools

This foundation positioned Reform to compete effectively in CRO-focused SaaS, scale PLG adoption, drive consistent pipeline contribution across FunnelEnvy's broader ecosystem, and expand into technical-adjacent audiences through programmable GTM infrastructure positioning.
