FAQs

If you’re wondering about it, I’ve probably answered it here

1. Which companies do you work with?

Mostly B2B SaaS and product-led startups—from early-stage founders to VC-backed scale-ups.

2. What’s your specialty?

End-to-end product marketing: positioning, GTM, lifecycle, and growth experiments. Execution, not just strategy.

3. How are you different from other advisors or agencies?

I don’t hand you a deck and walk away. Unlike agencies that focus only on ads or PR, I plug into your team to execute positioning, launches, lifecycle, and growth experiments end to end.

4. Can you write copy and content?

Yes. I write positioning docs, landing pages, launch assets, lifecycle emails, and campaign content.

5. Can you design?

I provide wireframes and design references, plus warm intros to trusted design partners when you need full creative.

6. Can you code?

I vibe-code websites, web apps, workflows and tools with Claude, n8n, Gemini, ChatGPT, Replit, etc. For more complex builds, I collaborate with your dev/product team to get things shipped smoothly.

7. Can you replace my agency?

In most cases, yes. My fractional PMaaS model (via TITAJA) covers strategy + execution, something agencies usually can’t.

8. Which teams do you work with?

Founders, execs (CMO, CRO, Head of Growth), and lean SaaS product/marketing teams.

9. Who will I be working with?

Me. No juniors, no outsourcing.

10. How do you work with my team?

Collaboratively. Depending on your engagement model, I either run execution for you (DFY), work alongside your team (DWY), or equip you with templates and frameworks to self-implement (DIY).

11. What’s your time commitment?

Consulting calls: 1 hour on-demand.
Weekly advisory: 3 calls/month (~12 hrs).
Fractional PMM: ~24 hrs/month, embedded with your team.

12. What’s your typical engagement?

Anywhere from a one-off power hour, to a 1–3 month consulting/advisory engagement, to ongoing fractional marketing leadership.

13. How does your pricing work?

Power Hour: $50 per call.
Weekly Advisory: $500/month.
Fractional Marketing Lead: $2,000/month (3-month recommended minimum).
All billed upfront, flexible cancellation depending on model.

14. What’s your process?

Discovery: We start with a call to understand your biggest growth gaps.
Proposal & Contract: I’ll send you a clear scope, terms, and contract so we’re aligned.
Strategy & Execution: I build the playbooks and run the campaigns, launches, or lifecycle flows.
Optimization: We review, measure, and iterate until we’ve scaled what works.

15. Can I do a trial?

Yes. You can start with a single Power Hour or short-term advisory before scaling up to a fractional engagement.

16. Do I need to pay upfront?

Yes. Power Hours and monthly retainers are paid upfront. Fractional engagements are invoiced monthly.

17. What’s the expected investment?

From $50 (consulting call) to $2,000/month (fractional lead). It’s flexible depending on scope and needs.

18. Is there a break clause?

Yes. Advisory retainers are cancel-anytime with 14 days’ notice. Fractional PMM requires a 3-month minimum but includes flexible terms.

19. Where are you based?

Lagos, Nigeria—working with global clients across US, Africa, and Europe.

20. Are you available for in-person meetings?

I have a remote-first approach to delivering my services, but if location and timing align, I can visit your office for a workshop to unpack specific challenges.
