3 reasons your revenue isn’t growing.

01

Your offer or positioning isn’t compelling enough

People see your ads, land on your page, or get your outbound message, but they don’t buy. The offer isn’t clear enough, or the positioning doesn’t make you the obvious choice. Fix this first. Nothing else works until it is.

02

You’re doing a bit of everything, and none of it compounds

You try outbound, then paid, then content, then lifecycle. But nobody figured out which one would actually move your numbers first. So growth depends on luck, not a plan.

03

Effort isn’t turning into revenue

Your reports show a lot of activity. But they don’t show new customers, closed deals, more pipeline, or more revenue from clients you already have. If you don’t know which of those is broken, you can’t fix it.

FAQs

If you’re wondering about it, I’ve probably answered it here

1. Which companies and teams do you work with?

Tech companies and agencies: B2B SaaS, consumer apps, and product-led startups alike, from early-stage founders to VC-backed scale-ups. I usually work directly with founders, execs (CMO, CRO, VP or Director of Marketing, Head of Growth), or small product and marketing teams.

2. What’s your specialty, and how are you different from other advisors or agencies?

I run your whole go-to-market: positioning, GTM infrastructure, outbound, paid, and lifecycle, as one system pointed at growth. I don’t hand you a deck and walk away. And unlike agencies that only run ads or PR, I join your team and execute all of it.

3. Can you write copy, design, and code?

Yes to all three. I write positioning docs, landing pages, launch assets, and lifecycle emails. For design, I provide wireframes and references, or connect you with design partners I trust. And I build websites, web apps, and workflows myself using AI tools like Claude, n8n, Gemini, ChatGPT, and Replit, working with your dev team on bigger builds.

4. Can you replace my agency, and who will I be working with?

In most cases, yes. Through Titaja, I offer product-marketing-as-a-service, which covers both strategy and execution, something most agencies can’t do. You work with me directly. No juniors, no outsourcing. Depending on what you need, I can run everything for you, work alongside your team, or set your team up to run it themselves.

5. What’s your typical engagement, and how much time does it take?

Most engagements start with the GTM Diagnostic, then move into the GTM System retainer once we’ve agreed what to build. If the gap is already obvious, some clients skip straight to ongoing execution.
Process: a discovery call, then a clear scope and contract, then strategy and execution, then review and adjust.
Time: the Diagnostic is a one-time, fixed engagement. GTM System and Embedded GTM Partner run on weekly execution with monthly reporting.

6. How does pricing work?

GTM Diagnostic: from $800, one-time, paid upfront.
GTM System: from $2,500/month, setup fee + retainer, 90-day minimum then month-to-month.
Embedded GTM Partner: from $4,000/month, same terms, deeper involvement.
We set final scope and pricing on the call, based on your buyer and channel mix. The Diagnostic is also a low-commitment way to start before you move into a retainer.

7. Where are you based, and can we meet in person?

Lagos, Nigeria, working with clients across the US, Africa, and Europe. I’m remote-first, but if the timing and location work out, I can visit your office for a workshop.
