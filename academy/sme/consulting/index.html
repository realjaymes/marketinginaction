How much you need depends on your product, price, and margin. The simple calculation: start with your margin per sale (selling price minus fulfillment cost), set a Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) target at 25% to 40% of that margin, then work backwards from your customer goal.

For example, if you sell at ₦20,000 with a ₦12,000 margin, your CPA target is around ₦4,000. To bring in 25 new customers in a month, you need ₦100,000 in ad spend, which generates ₦500,000 in revenue and around ₦200,000 in net profit after fulfillment and ad spend.
