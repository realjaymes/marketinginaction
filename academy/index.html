How MIA
Academy works

MIA Academy is not a one-off course. It’s a learning-to-career system designed to help you build skill, apply it in context, and turn it into opportunity.

Proactive Career Path

We focus on usable output rather than simple completion certificates. You build assets you can show employers.

1

Start with the right program

You begin by enrolling in the program that fits where you are right now. Whether it's a bootcamp for guided execution or a self-paced course for flexibility.

2

Learn with real context

You don't just consume content. You learn frameworks and approaches grounded in real product, growth, and GTM work.

3

Apply what you’re learning

As you go through a program, you apply what you learn to real scenarios, practical exercises, and portfolio-style work.

4

Get feedback from people doing the work

You don't learn in isolation. Through the MIA Community, you share work, ask questions, and get feedback from peers and practitioners.

5

Access the full MIA resource ecosystem

As a learner, you get ongoing access to templates, frameworks, playbooks, and supporting tools that evolve with the market.

6

Attend MIA events and deepen your network

MIA hosts focused virtual and in-person events designed to deepen trust and connect people doing similar work.

7

Become job-ready through proof of work

By applying what you learn, you build clear examples of execution and artifacts you can talk through with confidence.

8

Find opportunities through MIA Jobs

MIA Jobs connects marketers to roles based on execution skill and proof of work, not just resumes alone.

9

Get continuous career support

Your journey doesn't end after one program. You continue to get access to new resources, learning opportunities, and guidance.
