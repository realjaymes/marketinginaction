Colour candidates for the sales pages

Nothing here is live or applied. Each card shows how the element looks now and how it would look with colour, the pages it touches, and my call. Tick the ones you approve, then copy your picks from the bar at the bottom.

The rule I followed: gold stays on buttons only, colour goes on small labels and seals, never on card edges or big blocks, and each colour keeps one meaning on every page. Most of these slots already had a colour in the design that the mono setting turned to lilac or white.

Green: safety and value (guarantees, included, savings) Pink: bonuses and business owner pages Blue: marketer pages Purple: AI and credentials Gold: buttons only
